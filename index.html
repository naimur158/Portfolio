Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is the turnaround time?
Most videos are delivered within 2 to 5 days depending on length and complexity. Rush delivery is available on request — just mention your deadline when you reach out.
How many revisions do I get?
Every project includes multiple rounds of revisions until you're fully happy with the final edit.
What file formats do you accept and deliver?
Send raw footage via Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer in any common format (MP4, MOV, ProRes, AVI). Final videos are delivered as MP4 (H.264) in 1080p or 4K, plus vertical 9:16 versions for Shorts and Reels on request.
Do you write the script or just edit existing footage?
Both. I can edit your existing footage and script, or handle full script writing and AI voice-over so you only need to hand over a topic or outline.
Who owns the final video?
You do. Full ownership and commercial usage rights transfer to you once final payment is made — no royalties, no restrictions.
How do we communicate during the project?
Email, WhatsApp, or Discord — whichever you prefer. Expect replies within 1 hour during active projects and a progress update before each delivery.
